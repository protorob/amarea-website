Title: About Us

----

Introtitle: Where the sea meets a new way of living

----

Introtext:

A'Marea is a play on words inspired by *mare* — sea — and *marea* — the tide.

It's a name that reflects what we believe in: movement, natural rhythms and the freedom to flow with life rather than against it. A'Marea is a Mediterranean co-living home created for remote workers, travelers and creatives — a place to live, work, rest, create and connect. A place where you can feel at home, even when you're far from home.

----

Symboltitle: The Symbol

----

Symboltext:

Our logo is made of two connected water spirals, coming together to form an infinite shape.

Like drops of water creating ripples, the two spirals represent movement, connection and shared energy. Together, they reflect a community that keeps growing, evolving and renewing itself through balance, empathy and shared experiences.

----

Philosophytitle: Our Philosophy

----

Philosophyintro: At A'Marea, we believe life works better when there's room for both **focus and freedom, connection and solitude, productivity and slow living.**

----

Philosophyhome: A warm, thoughtful space designed to feel like home — just with the Mediterranean outside your door.

----

Philosophyflow: Spaces that help you get things done, while leaving room for long lunches, sea swims, creative moments and doing absolutely nothing.

----

Philosophybelonging: A naturally international community of remote workers, travelers and creatives. Come as you are, meet new people, share experiences and feel welcomed like family.

----

Philosophymediterranean: We take inspiration from the sea: slow down when you need to, move when you feel like it, stay curious and let things unfold naturally.
