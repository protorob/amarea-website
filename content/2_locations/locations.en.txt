Introtitle: Your Mediterranean playground

----

Introtext:

Just outside Syracuse, Fontane Bianche is one of the most beautiful corners of Sicily's south-eastern coast. Think crystal-clear water, white sandy beaches, hidden coves and long days spent outdoors.

And when you feel like exploring beyond the beach, there's so much more to discover — ancient cities, baroque villages, nature reserves, local food, vineyards and some of Sicily's most iconic landscapes.

Whether you're here for a few days or settling in for a while, there's always somewhere new to go, taste, swim or explore.

Areaguidetitle: Around A'Marea

----

Beachdaystext: Start with Fontane Bianche's beautiful sandy beach, then explore the smaller coves and quieter spots along the coast. Swim, snorkel, paddle or simply find your favourite place to watch the sunset.

----

Syracuseortigiatext: Wander through ancient streets, discover Greek and Roman history, stop for an espresso in a sunny piazza or stay for aperitivo and dinner. Ortigia comes alive as the sun goes down.

----

Baroquesicilytext: Discover Noto, Modica, Ragusa and the towns of the Val di Noto — famous for their beautiful architecture, golden stone and unmistakable Sicilian character.

----

Natureadventuretext: Explore the Vendicari Nature Reserve or the Cavagrande canyon, hike along the coast, discover hidden beaches or head inland for a change of scenery. For a bigger adventure, Mount Etna is just a road trip away.

----

Foodwinetext: Fresh seafood, arancini, pasta, granita, cannoli, local wines and seasonal produce. Sicily is best experienced around a table — preferably outside, with good company.

----

Slowdaystext: Sometimes the best plan is no plan. A morning swim, a long lunch, a book in the shade and a sunset aperitivo. This is the Mediterranean rhythm we love.
