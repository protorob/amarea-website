  question: How do I get to Fontane Bianche from the airport?
  answer: >
    A'Marea is located in Fontane Bianche, just outside Syracuse, around
    70 km from Catania Fontanarossa Airport. Address: Via Bordighera, 4 —
    Fontane Bianche, Syracuse. By car it's approximately 50 minutes from
    Catania Airport, 15 minutes from Syracuse, and 20 minutes from Noto.
    The Fontane Bianche railway station is a 10-minute walk away, one
    stop from Syracuse and two stops from Noto. Local bus lines 21 and 22
    connect Fontane Bianche to Syracuse, and Interbus connects Catania to
    Syracuse. Free street parking is available directly in front of
    A'Marea.
  category: location

  question: Can I rent the entire property for a private event?
