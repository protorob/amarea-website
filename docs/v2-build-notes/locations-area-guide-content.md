**Title:** Around A'Marea

**Beach days & hidden coves**
Start with Fontane Bianche's beautiful sandy beach, then explore the
smaller coves and quieter spots along the coast. Swim, snorkel, paddle or
simply find your favourite place to watch the sunset.

**Syracuse & Ortigia**
Wander through ancient streets, discover Greek and Roman history, stop for
an espresso in a sunny piazza or stay for aperitivo and dinner. Ortigia
comes alive as the sun goes down.

**Baroque Sicily**
Discover Noto, Modica, Ragusa and the towns of the Val di Noto — famous
for their beautiful architecture, golden stone and unmistakable Sicilian
character.

**Nature & adventure**
Explore the Vendicari Nature Reserve or the Cavagrande canyon, hike along
the coast, discover hidden beaches or head inland for a change of scenery.
For a bigger adventure, Mount Etna is just a road trip away.

**Food, wine & local life**
Fresh seafood, arancini, pasta, granita, cannoli, local wines and seasonal
produce. Sicily is best experienced around a table — preferably outside,
with good company.

**Slow days by the sea**
Sometimes the best plan is no plan. A morning swim, a long lunch, a book
in the shade and a sunset aperitivo. This is the Mediterranean rhythm we
love.
