## The Spaces

A’Marea is made to flow with your day. Find a quiet corner to focus, a sunny spot to slow down, or a shared table where a quick hello turns into a long dinner.

### The Living Room

The glass heart of the house. A bright, air-conditioned space at the centre of A’Marea that shifts with the rhythm of the day, a calm place to work and focus during daylight hours, and a warm space to connect, share and unwind together in the evening. 

### The Kitchen & outside kitchen

A fully equipped kitchen and shared dining area, with the option to cook indoors or in the outdoor kitchen surrounded by the garden — made for cooking together, long lunches and dinners that somehow last all evening.

### The Garden & Terrace

The best part might just be outside. A Mediterranean garden, spacious terrace, sea views  — with plenty of space for morning coffee, sunset drinks and everything in between.
