## The apartments 

- Zagara
  A Sicilian Home in the Heart of Syracuse
  Gallery: pictures of the apartment

  Discover Zagara, the most spacious apartment at A’Marea City Apartments. A private and fully equipped home designed for those who want the comfort and intimacy of living on their own, while staying right in the heart of Syracuse. Just steps away from all daily conveniences, the local market, the port of Santa Lucia, the sea, and only a short walk from Ortigia, Zagara offers the perfect balance between city life and Mediterranean escape. With two spacious bedrooms, a cosy living room, a charming mezzanine library and a fully equipped kitchen opening onto the shared courtyard, Zagara is made for slow mornings, easy living and feeling truly at home in Sicily.
