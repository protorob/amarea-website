**A’Marea — About Us**

## **Where the sea meets a new way of living**

**LOGO**

A’Marea is a play on words inspired by *mare* — sea — and *marea* — the tide.

It’s a name that reflects what we believe in: movement, natural rhythms and the freedom to flow with life rather than against it.

A’Marea is a Mediterranean co-living home created for remote workers, travelers and creatives — a place to live, work, rest, create and connect. A place where you can feel at home, even when you’re far from home.

### **The Symbol**

Our logo is made of two connected water spirals, coming together to form an infinite shape.

Like drops of water creating ripples, the two spirals represent movement, connection and shared energy. Together, they reflect a community that keeps growing, evolving and renewing itself through balance, empathy and shared experiences.

### **Our Philosophy**

At A’Marea, we believe life works better when there’s room for both **focus and freedom, connection and solitude, productivity and slow living.**

#### **Home**

A warm, thoughtful space designed to feel like home — just with the Mediterranean outside your door.

#### **Flow**

Spaces that help you get things done, while leaving room for long lunches, sea swims, creative moments and doing absolutely nothing.

#### **Belonging**

A naturally international community of remote workers, travelers and creatives. Come as you are, meet new people, share experiences and feel welcomed like family.

#### **The Mediterranean Way**

We take inspiration from the sea: slow down when you need to, move when you feel like it, stay curious and let things unfold naturally.

Fontane Bianche & Syracuse

## **Your Mediterranean playground**

Just outside Syracuse, Fontane Bianche is one of the most beautiful corners of Sicily’s south-eastern coast. Think crystal-clear water, white sandy beaches, hidden coves and long days spent outdoors.

And when you feel like exploring beyond the beach, there’s so much more to discover — ancient cities, baroque villages, nature reserves, local food, vineyards and some of Sicily’s most iconic landscapes.

Whether you’re here for a few days or settling in for a while, there’s always somewhere new to go, taste, swim or explore.

## **Around A’Marea**

### **Beach days & hidden coves**

Start with Fontane Bianche’s beautiful sandy beach, then explore the smaller coves and quieter spots along the coast. Swim, snorkel, paddle or simply find your favourite place to watch the sunset.

### **Syracuse & Ortigia**

Wander through ancient streets, discover Greek and Roman history, stop for an espresso in a sunny piazza or stay for aperitivo and dinner. Ortigia comes alive as the sun goes down.

### **Baroque Sicily**

Discover Noto, Modica, Ragusa and the towns of the Val di Noto — famous for their beautiful architecture, golden stone and unmistakable Sicilian character.

### **Nature & adventure**

Explore the Vendicari Nature Reserve or the Cavagrande canyon, hike along the coast, discover hidden beaches or head inland for a change of scenery. For a bigger adventure, Mount Etna is just a road trip away.

### **Food, wine & local life**

Fresh seafood, arancini, pasta, granita, cannoli, local wines and seasonal produce. Sicily is best experienced around a table — preferably outside, with good company.

### **Slow days by the sea**

Sometimes the best plan is no plan. A morning swim, a long lunch, a book in the shade and a sunset aperitivo. This is the Mediterranean rhythm we love.

**How do I get to Fontane Bianche from the airport?**

A’Marea is located in Fontane Bianche, just outside Syracuse, around 70 km from Catania Fontanarossa Airport.

📍 **Address:** Via Bordighera, 4 — Fontane Bianche, Syracuse

A’Marea is easy to reach by car, train or bus, whether you’re coming from Catania Airport, Syracuse or Noto.

**🚗 From Catania Airport****
** A’Marea is approximately **50 minutes by car** from Catania Fontanarossa Airport. Follow the direction of Syracuse, then continue towards Fontane Bianche.

**🚗 From Syracuse****
** Fontane Bianche is approximately **15 minutes by car** from Syracuse, heading towards Noto.

**🚗 From Noto****
** A’Marea is approximately **20 minutes by car** from Noto, heading towards Syracuse.

**🚆 By train****
** The **Fontane Bianche railway station** is just a short walk from A’Marea, approximately **10 minutes on foot**. It is one stop from Syracuse and two stops from Noto.

**🚌 By bus from Syracuse****
** You can reach Fontane Bianche from Syracuse using local bus lines **21 (Stazione–Fontane Bianche–Cassibile)** or **22 (Stazione–Cassibile–Fontane Bianche)**. Buses depart from Via Rubino, near Piazza della Stazione. The **Fontane Bianche** stop is just a few minutes’ walk from A’Marea.

**🚌 From Catania by bus****
** You can take an **Interbus** service from Catania to Syracuse, then continue to Fontane Bianche using local bus lines 21 or 22.

**🅿️ Parking****
** Free street parking is available directly in front of A’Marea, making it easy and convenient if you’re arriving by car.
